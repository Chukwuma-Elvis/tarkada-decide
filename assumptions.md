# Appendix — Stated Assumptions

Per the brief's rule ("if you need something that is not in this brief, state your assumption where you use it and carry on"), assumptions are also restated inline wherever they are used. This file collects them in one place.

1. **Country/currency context.** The brief's use of naira and kobo places this in Nigeria; the National Secondary Certificate Council is treated as a fictional body structurally analogous to a national examinations council, and no real-world fact about any actual such body is assumed beyond what the brief states.
2. **PIN ownership model.** A result-checker PIN is sold anonymously through banks, agents and the website and is not tied to a candidate at the point of sale — it is bound to whichever exam number the holder supplies at first use. *(Used in: Data Model, API Contract.)*
3. **Payment partner capabilities.** The partner can charge a card synchronously and returns either a definite success/decline or an indeterminate timeout. It is assumed to expose either a transaction-lookup-by-merchant-reference call or, failing that, a daily settlement file, for reconciliation — the brief states it has no idempotency support but says nothing about lookup or settlement capability. *(Used in: PIN Purchase Fix.)*
4. **Officer authentication.** Council officers who record amendments and run searches authenticate via an internal identity system already operated by the Council; its detailed design is out of scope. *(Used in: Amendment workflow, ADR-006.)*
5. **USSD/SMS delivery.** Candidate access via USSD/SMS is delivered through a telecom aggregator gateway under commercial contract; no specific aggregator is named. *(Used in: Protocol choice, Transport security.)*
6. **Team composition.** Of the six engineers (including the author), the WBS assumes: 3 backend/platform engineers (one doubling as the candidate-channel/USSD specialist), 1 data engineer/DBA, 1 engineer covering integrations (payment partner, employers' association, schools), 1 QA/test engineer. Not specified in the brief.
7. **Legacy system.** The 2009 system is assumed to hold the 18-year archive in some queryable form that can be exported and migrated into the new data model; no detail of its technology is assumed beyond "nobody on staff wrote it."
8. **Certificate identity.** Each candidate receives one certificate per sitting, covering all subjects sat that sitting, identified by a certificate number distinct from the exam number.
9. **Result-check response size.** A single check response (9 subject results plus minimal metadata) is estimated at under 2KB of JSON — small enough that on a 2G link, transmission time is dominated by round-trip/handshake latency, not payload size. Labelled estimate, not measured.
10. **Employers' association integration pattern.** The association's 500,000 monthly verification calls arrive as individual synchronous API calls from its own systems (not a nightly batch file), consistent with "verification calls" in the brief.
